Rise of the Clans

A grand-strategy game of the Sengoku period — war, trade, diplomacy, honour, and intrigue in the struggle to rule Japan.

You lead a rival daimyō on the national map. Defeat five AI clans across six roads to victory — conquest, wealth, culture, honour, legitimacy, or the shadow war.

A playable digital adaptation of the tabletop rulebook “Shōgun: Rise of the Clans — Master Edition v3.2”.
